Welcome to Sankofa For Peace

Sankofa for Peace is a nonprofit organization in Washington State establisted in 2019.


Our Mission

Sankofa for Peace is to create a safe, supportive, and thriving community where vulnerable individuals and families have access to the resources and opportunities they need to improve their lives. We are driven by the belief that no one should face life’s challenges alone. Through our work, we aim to:

  • Support vulnerable individuals and families by providing essential resources such as food, clothing, and financial assistance.

  • Foster educational success for children by providing tutoring, school supplies, and after-school programs that promote academic and personal growth.

  • Promote mental health and wellness through accessible counseling, support services, and mental health awareness campaigns.

We envision a world where every person has the chance to live a healthy, happy, and fulfilling life, regardless of their background or circumstances. Through our ongoing efforts, we hope to bring hope, healing, and opportunity to those who need it most.
